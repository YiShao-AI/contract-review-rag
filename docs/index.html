The operating problem

Status is visible. Contract language is still buried.

Before

An operating board could show which agreements were active or approaching renewal, but a user still had to find the correct file, locate the clause, interpret the renewal terms, and calculate notice deadlines by hand.

With the knowledge system

A user asks the business question in plain English, receives a grounded response, opens the supporting passage, and can trace the answer back to the original document before acting.

My role: problem framing, requirements, data-readiness analysis, retrieval and citation design, implementation, evaluation, and workflow validation.
End-to-end workflow

From mixed documents to a verifiable answer

IngestAccept PDF, DOCX, and TXT; route scans and filled forms through OCR.
StructurePreserve document metadata and split content on clause-aware boundaries.
RetrieveCombine dense-vector similarity with SQLite FTS5 keyword search.
AnswerGenerate from retrieved context and attach source citations.
VerifyOpen the cited passage and original document before the business action.
Technical capabilities

Designed for retrieval quality and source truth

Hybrid search

FAISS vector retrieval catches paraphrase; SQLite FTS5 preserves exact names, clauses, amounts, and business terms. Reciprocal-rank fusion combines both candidate lists.

FAISSFTS5RRF

Clause-aware ingestion

Document structure, pages, sections, and filenames stay attached to chunks. OCR recovers scanned content and values present only in a visual form overlay.

OCRPDFDOCX

Traceable answers

Citations are not decorative labels: each one resolves to retrieved source text and can open the relevant document context in the interface.

Product decisions

What makes the workflow usable

DecisionReasonBusiness effect
Source-first interfaceA fluent answer is insufficient when the user must act on contract terms.Verification and correction are part of the normal workflow.
Hybrid retrievalContract questions mix paraphrase with exact clause language and identifiers.Fewer blind spots than a vector-only or keyword-only search.
Derived values are labeledNotice deadlines may be calculated from multiple clauses rather than stated directly.Users can distinguish document facts from system calculations.
Local-first defaultContract content may require a controlled processing boundary.Deployment can keep corpus and inference inside the operating environment.
